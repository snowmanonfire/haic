HAIC — The Himalayan AI Corridor Project
Compute, at altitude

The world needs more compute.
The mountains were built for it.

HAIC is building a connected network of AI compute facilities across Nepal — drawing on abundant green hydropower and the natural cooling of high, clean Himalayan air. One corridor. Many sites. Compute that scales with the world's demand.

Every leap in artificial intelligence rests on one thing: the ability to compute. HAIC exists to supply it — cleanly, efficiently, and at scale — from one of the few places on earth where nature does the hardest work for you.

Powered by green energy

Nepal's hydropower is clean, renewable and abundant. HAIC sites run on it — turning the country's greatest natural resource into the fuel for global intelligence.

Cooled by the Himalayas

At altitude, the air is cool, thin and clean. HAIC uses it directly — free-air cooling that cuts energy, cost and complexity that conventional datacenters cannot escape.

Built as an optimized network

Not one facility, but a corridor of them. A repeatable model that grows site by site, designed to serve customers of every kind, anywhere in the world.

The corridor ahead

A single, expanding network of high-altitude compute — ready to serve every class of customer, across the globe, in whatever arrangement the future demands.
